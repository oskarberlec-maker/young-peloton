Who is responsible

Young Peloton is an independent European junior cycling calendar. Controller: Oskar Berlec, Slovenia. For privacy enquiries and requests, email oskar.berlec@gmail.com; do not post personal information in public GitHub issues.

Purpose of the project and race information

Young Peloton is a non-commercial project. Race information is used solely to prepare and maintain a useful cycling race calendar and its related discovery, planning and race-update features. The operator does not intend to earn money from the project.

This describes the purpose of using race information. Technical data and personal data associated with optional notifications, website delivery and correspondence are processed for the specific purposes explained below.

Saved races and storage on your device

You can use the calendar and save races without an account. Saved race IDs and preferences are stored in your browser. Session storage helps restore your position in the calendar. The installed app uses a service worker and browser caches. Browser and installed-app storage can be separate. Locally stored data remains until you remove it, clear website data or your browser removes it. Session storage normally ends with the browser session.

You can remove saved races on the Saved races page and export a backup before clearing data. Backups and calendar files downloaded to your device remain under your control. Local saving alone does not synchronize your list to an online account. Enabling push notifications adds the server processing described below.

Optional race notifications

When you enable alerts, your browser asks for notification permission. The website sends your push subscription endpoint, technical encryption keys and saved 2026 race IDs to the notification server. It stores the subscription, protected fingerprints of the race IDs, creation/update dates and a record of notifications sent, in private Vercel Blob storage. These identifiers are not treated as anonymous merely because no name or email is requested.

The purpose is to send relevant alerts when a saved 2026 race receives a verified 2027 date and to avoid duplicate messages. The proposed legal basis is your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). Providing this information is optional; without it, push alerts cannot be delivered and the calendar remains available.

You can withdraw by selecting “Turn off 2027 alerts” on Saved races. This requests server deletion and revokes the browser subscription. Clearing browser storage alone does not necessarily delete the server record. If a deletion request fails, use the private controller contact. Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing. The current service removes endpoints reported as invalid during delivery attempts; it does not implement a fixed inactivity expiry. Your rights

Depending on the processing and applicable conditions, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction and portability of your personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can withdraw consent at any time. Contact the controller privately; we may need proportionate information to identify your record. Do not send push subscription keys through a public issue.

You may complain to the Slovenian Information Commissioner, Informacijski pooblaščenec, or another competent supervisory authority. The reviewed calendar and notification code does not use automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.
